Produce Storage & Freshness Tips

🥔 Root Vegetables

Store sweet potatoes and yams in a cool, dark, well-ventilated basket—avoid plastic bags to prevent moisture buildup.

🥬 Leafy Greens

Wash and gently dry greens immediately upon delivery, wrap in a damp cloth or paper towel, and store in the refrigerator crisper.

🍅 Vine Tomatoes

Keep ripe tomatoes at room temperature out of direct sunlight to preserve their natural sweetness and firm texture.
